OmniCoreSDR for MATLAB

Use OmniCoreSDR from MATLAB and Simulink: capture, transmit, timed RX/TX, and full duplex

OmniCoreSDR is based on the AD9361: 2 receive and 2 transmit channels, 70 MHz – 6 GHz, sample rates of 2.5 – 61.44 MSPS, with hardware timestamps and PPS time alignment. This toolbox calls the OmniCore Host runtime library directly through a MEX file, does not depend on SoapySDR, needs no MATLAB toolboxes, and supports MATLAB R2018a and later.
