Examples

Complete programs in the toolbox's examples folder

First add the examples folder to the path:

addpath(fullfile(fileparts(which('omnicore_mex')), 'examples'))

The application examples all have a 'Simulate', true option: they run on a software-simulated channel without a device, which makes it easy to understand the flow first. See the full description of each example with help, e.g. help fm_radio. The examples only use base MATLAB and need no toolboxes.

The loopback examples default to 433.92 MHz with very low transmit power (TxGain of 30 dB for fm_loopback and 40 dB for video_link, i.e. 59.75 / 49.75 dB attenuation). Ideally connect TX1 to RX1 through an attenuator (30–60 dB); when using antennas, follow your local radio regulations. Over-the-air RX/TX at 2.4 GHz gets interrupted by Wi-Fi frames (a few milliseconds long and stronger than this signal), so video_link loses many frames there; a cable or 433.92 MHz avoids this.

Applications

fm_radio — FM broadcast radio

Listen to 88–108 MHz FM broadcasts (mono), with live displays of the RF spectrum, the composite (MPX) spectrum, and the audio waveform. Demonstrates: offset tuning and digital mixing, polyphase decimation filters, phase-discriminator demodulation, de-emphasis, and real-time processing during continuous receive.

fm_radio(98.7e6)                                   % FM antenna on RX1, plays for 30 s
a = fm_radio(98.7e6, 'Seconds', 10, 'Audio', false);
fm_radio(98.7e6, 'Simulate', true)                 % two simulated stations

fm_loopback — FM transmit-and-receive loopback

On the same board, TX1 transmits FM audio at 433.92 MHz (MATLAB's built-in handel by default), and RX1 receives, demodulates, and plays it; at the end, the received audio is aligned with the transmitted audio to report end-to-end latency and audio SNR. Demonstrates: full-duplex RX/TX in one loop, TX headroom, offsetting the TX/RX LOs, and link measurement.

r = fm_loopback('Seconds', 5);
fprintf('Latency %.1f ms, SNR %.1f dB\n', 1e3*r.DelaySeconds, r.SnrDb);
fm_loopback('Simulate', true, 'Channel', struct('Loss', 70))

video_link — digital video link

Reads a video frame by frame (MATLAB's built-in xylophone.mp4 by default), compresses it to JPEG, sends it from TX1 as QPSK bursts, and decodes and displays it on RX1, while showing the constellation, EVM, frequency offset, and throughput. Demonstrates: root-raised-cosine shaping and matched filtering, Zadoff-Chu preamble sync, frequency offset estimation, pilot phase tracking, checksums, and transmitting at hardware timestamps and cutting the bursts out of the RX stream. Getting every bit of a frame right needs about 15 dB SNR.

r = video_link('Seconds', 10);
video_link('Simulate', true)
video_link('Simulate', true, 'Channel', struct('Loss', 64))   % frame loss at low SNR

sdr_scope — simple oscilloscope and spectrum analyzer

A graphical interface: I/Q, magnitude, or phase waveforms (rising/falling edge trigger, level, time span), averaged spectrum with peak hold, and controls on the right for changing center frequency, sample rate, gain/AGC, and channel live. Demonstrates: repeatedly taking fresh snapshots with receive, and changing device parameters from UI callbacks.

sdr_scope
sdr_scope('Frequency', 433.92e6, 'SampleRate', 5e6)
sdr_scope('Simulate', true)                        % tone + periodic chirp

How the examples are structured

The application examples share a few small functions in examples/private. Private functions can only be called by files in examples; to use them in your own programs, copy them into your folder:

sdr_linkA common interface for RX1/TX1: the real device (RxStream, TxStream, hardware time) or a simulated channel (delay, attenuation, LO offset, noise, ADC clipping)
fir_lowpass, fir_decimator, fir_decimateWindowed-sinc low-pass design and polyphase decimation (called in blocks, with results identical to filter followed by decimation)
fm_transmitter, fm_modulate, fm_receiver, fm_demodulateWideband FM modulation and demodulation chains
psd_dbAveraged power spectrum (dBFS)
audio_sinkPlay while receiving: seamless playback with audioDeviceWriter when Audio Toolbox / DSP System Toolbox is available, otherwise segmented playback with audioplayer
